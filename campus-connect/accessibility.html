Accessibility

Campus-Connect is built to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. If something does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it.

Display settings

Use the controls in the page header. Your choices are remembered on this device.

  • Text size: three steps, from default to 136%.
  • Dark mode: follows your system setting until you change it.
  • Reduced motion: animations are off when your system asks for it.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Tab and Shift+Tab move between links and controls.
  • Enter activates links and buttons.
  • The first Tab stop on every page is a link that skips to the main content.

What we built in

  • Semantic landmarks: header, navigation, main and footer.
  • Visible focus outlines on every interactive element.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in both themes.
  • Form fields with permanent labels.
  • Search results announced to screen readers through a live region.
  • A layout that reflows to a 320px width without sideways scrolling.

Contact

Email access@campus-connect.example and describe the page and the problem. We reply within three working days.

Campus-Connect is a student project. Course details are sample data. Report an accessibility problem at access@campus-connect.example.
